Kinestasis changelog

0.1.5 Aug 13, 2026
  • GIF export. Right-click any shot. One frame per still with delays from the real cadence, ramps included, looping forever. 480, 640, or 960 px. Plays everywhere.
  • Drag frames out. Drag the player frame into Finder, Messages, Mail, or an app icon and it lands as a full-resolution graded JPEG, not a thumbnail.
  • Previews build once, ever. Generated previews are cached on disk. The second import of a folder, and every one after, is near-instant and silent.
  • While previews generate, the grid shows placeholders that fill in as shots finish, and the rest of your Mac stays responsive.
  • Importing a folder with videos could hitch video playback and typing across the whole machine, and one bad video could hang the app until force quit. Video processing now runs one clip at a time and bad clips are skipped.
  • The camera's in-camera RAW conversions no longer splice into their source burst as out-of-order frames; they go to Singles.
  • Keyboard and controls no longer slow down over a long session.
  • Preview generation could freeze partway through.
0.1.4 Aug 13, 2026
  • Develop View. Process one burst at a time: big player, grading at the side, the skimmable shot strip below, prev/next with the arrow keys, and key hints that light up as you press. The top bar now reads VIEW: Bin, Develop, Assemble. Cmd+F jumps to Develop in fullscreen; Esc steps back out.
  • Preview quality tiers. A Preview control by the export button: Draft, Balanced, or High. High develops the real RAW at 2560 px in the background and playback sharpens as frames finish. Paused frames always refine to near-full quality. Importing shows a Generating previews bar; your clicks and scrubs always jump the queue.
  • Grading overhaul. Highlights now goes the right direction (up brightens, down recovers). New Whites and Blacks sliders. A tone curve editor below the sliders. Contrast is a proper S-curve with a much wider range. Wobble is actually visible now, breathing exposure and contrast together.
  • Hold on This Still, finished. Hold length in frames, separate ease in and ease out, and an optional "then skip" that drops stills when the hold releases, as if the cadence kept running underneath.
  • JPEG bursts previewed from the camera's 160 px thumbnail no matter the quality setting. They now decode properly at every tier; the brief shrunken low-quality flash while scrubbing is gone.
  • Playback and scrubbing no longer freeze the app. Seven separate causes found and fixed; a two-minute stress run over the full 221-burst archive now shows near-zero interruptions.
  • Long bursts no longer degrade after the first playback loop.
0.1.3 Aug 13, 2026
  • Stills selection. Press M to mark a still. Marked stills export with the batch into a Stills folder as graded full-resolution JPEGs, with options to include the unmodified originals and RAW files.
  • Hold on This Still. Pick a hold length and easing in the ramp section; the speed ramp is generated for you.
  • Press I and O together to clear a shot's in/out points. K+L steps one frame forward, J+K one frame back.
0.1.2 Aug 13, 2026
  • Every filmstrip shows its capture rate (like ~8 fps capture), measured from the first ten stills so buffer slowdown does not skew it.
  • Frame skip is its own setting. A SKIP control in the project bar sets the default; any shot can override it. It combines with as-shot speeds and fixed frames per still.
  • The As Shot menu shows the effective frames per still at the project rate, 23.976 included.
  • Hovering a shot previews it without changing the selection. Click to select; the player returns to your selection when the cursor leaves.
  • Trimmed head and tail are darkened on filmstrips, with a kept-range strip in the player.
  • The export sheet remembers your last settings and warns before overwriting: Keep Both, Replace Existing, or Back.
  • Large imports no longer slow the whole machine down.
  • New projects default to 23.976 fps.
  • The RAW/JPEG source toggle is also in the shot right-click menu.
0.1.1 Aug 13, 2026
  • Fixed the launch crash on any machine other than the one that built the app: the packaged app could not find its resources. Every build is now launch-tested under clean-machine conditions before it ships.
0.1.0 Aug 11, 2026
  • First release. Burst grouping from EXIF capture gaps, JPEG and OEM RAW with RAW+JPEG pairs, three timing modes, per-shot trim and speed ramps, camera-raw grading with LUTs, grain, and exposure wobble, direct timeline playback, and batch export to ProRes, H.264, or HEVC with an optional FCPXML sidecar. Notarized DMG.
