0.1.2 August 13, 2026 build 64

A day of hands-on direction: measurement, control, and courtesy.

Capture rate, measured and visible

  • Every filmstrip shows its approximate capture rate under the filename, like ~8 fps capture. Measured from the first ten stills, so the Fuji's buffer slowdown never skews it and the Sony's steady 10 fps reads exactly.
  • The As Shot menu now states what real time means for the shot at the current project rate: Real time (about 3.0 frames/still at 23.976). Recomputed when the rate changes; NTSC rationals are exact.
  • Shot metadata lines include the rate and wrap instead of truncating.

Frame skip, unlocked

  • Frame skip left the timing modes. It is its own setting now: a SKIP control in the project bar sets the default for every shot, and any shot can override it from its menus.
  • Skip stacks with everything: every 2nd still at half speed, every 3rd with 4 frames per still, whatever the shot calls for.
  • Old project files migrate automatically; a saved frame-skip timing becomes fixed timing plus the skip.

Selection that stays put

  • Hovering a shot previews it in the player and hands it the transport keys, but the inspector keeps the shot you clicked. Leave the grid and the player returns to your selection, right where it was.
  • Clicking a shot you are skimming adopts the skim position as the new selection.

Trims you can see

  • I/O trims darken the cut-off head and tail on every filmstrip card, in both the shots grid and the Assemble bin.
  • The player gains a kept-range strip: full shot in dark, kept range in amber. Trimmed stills never play in the loop.

Exports with a memory and manners

  • The export sheet remembers your last codec, resolution, bitrate, sidecar choice, and destination.
  • Nothing overwrites silently anymore. Existing files raise a choice: Keep Both (numbered names), Replace Existing, or Back.

Fixed

  • Large imports no longer drag the whole machine down. The EXIF probe leaves two cores free at background priority, thumbnails queue three at a time, and skim decodes run through a small newest-first pool so the frame under the cursor still lands instantly.
  • Skimming a trimmed shot showed frames offset by the trim amount; the skim now reads the same list playback uses.
  • RAW/JPEG source toggle is discoverable from the shot right-click menu, not just the inspector.

Changed

  • New projects default to 23.976 fps.
0.1.1 August 13, 2026 build 57

The one that launches on your Mac, not just ours.

Fixed

  • 0.1.0 crashed at launch on any machine but the one that built it: the packaged app looked for its resources in two places that only exist on the dev Mac. Resources now resolve from inside the app, and a missing bundle degrades gracefully instead of crashing.
  • Every future build is launch-tested in a clean-machine condition before it can ship; a repeat of this bug now fails the build instead of the user.
0.1.0 August 11, 2026 build 55

First notarized release.

Everything, from zero

  • Drag in a folder of stills; shots group themselves by capture-time gaps, with singles set aside and days as sections.
  • JPEG and OEM RAW (Fujifilm RAF, Sony ARW, and the rest of the CIRAWFilter family), with RAW+JPEG pairs collapsed to one still and switchable.
  • Three timing modes: fixed frames per still, as-shot cadence (the signature), frame skip. Per-shot trim and speed ramps.
  • Camera-raw grading with LUTs, film grain, and exposure wobble; live-graded hover skim and space/JKL transport.
  • Shots play directly on a timeline with no pre-render, and batch-export to ProRes 422/HQ/4444, H.264, or HEVC at native resolution, with an optional FCPXML sidecar and a parallel pipeline that develops RAF at about 9 fps with grain.
  • Dark, on-brand interface end to end. Notarized, stapled, Gatekeeper-clean DMG.
